# HackAI 2026: what the organisers ask for

Source: https://hackai.my/ and https://hackai.my/tracks/cyber-defence (check again for changes).

## Key dates
- Proposal submission closes **15 Oct 2026**. The registration page said 31 Oct, so confirm with the organisers.
- Finals: **7 Nov 2026**, UTAR Kampar, live presentation.
- Prize pool: RM5,000.

## Proposal (1–2 pages) should cover
The problem and target user, the agent workflow, architecture, tools, data flow, safeguards, limitations, and scope.

## Judging weights
| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Technical implementation | 25% |
| Problem relevance | 20% |
| Effective use of agentic AI | 20% |
| Cybersecurity value | 20% |
| Presentation and usability | 15% |

## Track 01 brief: what the demo must show
A clear problem and user, the inputs and evidence used, the agent workflow (tool calls), a defensive action, human approval before consequential actions, and results with known limitations.
